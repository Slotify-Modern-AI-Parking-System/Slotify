About Slotify

Slotify is an AI-powered solution designed to optimize urban parking experiences by leveraging OpenCV, AI models, and Google APIs. Our system detects available parking slots in real time, guides users to their assigned slots, and provides parking lot owners with actionable analytics.

Privacy and Data Usage

License Plate Recognition: Our system uses automated license plate recognition (ALPR) with OpenCV and Tesseract OCR for vehicle tracking and slot assignment. This data is used solely for parking management purposes.

Location Data: We collect location information to provide navigation services and optimize parking slot allocation using Google Maps API.

Analytics: Usage data is collected to provide insights on parking utilization, peak times, and revenue reporting for parking lot owners.
